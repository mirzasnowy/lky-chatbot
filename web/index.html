What Would Lee Kuan Yew Do?

A chatbot that answers in Lee Kuan Yew's voice, grounded only in his own public speeches (13 speeches, 1965–2011, ~31,000 words). Every answer cites the speech it comes from. If a question is not covered by the speeches, it says so instead of inventing an answer.

Built for Section 3 of the 99 Group AI Aptitude Challenge. Code, corpus, prompts and evaluation: github.com/mirzasnowy/lky-chatbot
